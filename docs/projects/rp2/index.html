READ THE INTERVALS CAREFULLY

95% intervals are seeded circular seven-day block bootstrap summaries of mean absolute errors, not forecast intervals. They depend on the resampling assumptions and can mislead on short or shifting series. Positive paired difference favors the 7-day baseline. A later target date does not prove a series or domain was absent from pretraining.
