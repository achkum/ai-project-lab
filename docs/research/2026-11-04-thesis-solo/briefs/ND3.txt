ND3: Show where a small flood mapper is unsure about the shoreline
4 November 2026 — WITHDRAW

Problem
A fast flood mask can be wrong around boundaries and small flooded areas.

Example
Two maps agree about a large flooded field but disagree along a road; the study would expose those uncertain pixels.

Research question
Does a learned local boundary correction/rejection stage improve event-held-out flood mapping at a fixed CPU inference budget?

What you would build/train
Would adapt a small segmentation decoder or boundary head; compare to compact U-Net and frozen-feature linear heads.

Comparisons
Compact U-Net Prithvi-derived Tiny student; frozen and fully trained versions Threshold calibration without a learned correction stage

Evaluation
Nested event-level splits; thresholds and model selection must exclude the held-out event. Report boundary F1, IoU, risk/coverage and peak RAM, not only average segmentation accuracy.

Data and terms
Complete official label TIFF and S1/S2 chips downloaded. Label decoded as 512 by 512 with -1/0/1 values. Both official STAC collections declare proprietary. Unresolved: official source and label STAC say proprietary, while other descriptions/mirrors imply openness. GitHub issue 18 remains open; all two replies read, last 2026-09-15 asks the same clarification. No maintainer resolution found.

CPU plan
Not qualified. A small head on 128-pixel tiles could be tested locally, but accessible bytes do not grant reuse rights.

Semester scope
Would need reproduction, strict event-split correction, adaptation, CPU profiling and failure analysis; no work is authorized while rights remain unclear.

Closest work
October 2026 Prithvi-to-Tiny paper already studies compact flood segmentation, boundaries, entropy and CPU speed; U-Net is a strong alternative.

Proposed contribution
Nested selection and actionable boundary rejection would need separate evaluation; generic small-model flood mapping is already established.

Research connection
LTU Computer Vision lists the Swedish Space Data Lab; geospatial image analysis is substantively relevant.

Limits
No thesis endorsement; no right to reuse the selected dataset. Proposed fit only: no examiner, supervisor, project membership or resource approval.

Risks
Unresolved data rights Few independent flood events Foundation pretraining exposure and hyperparameter leakage

Output
Licence conflict evidence and withdrawn lead.

Verdict
