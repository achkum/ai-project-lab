ND2: Tell mosquito species apart without learning the recording site
4 November 2026 — WITHDRAW

Problem
A model may identify the microphone or collection setting instead of the insect.

Example
A recording from a new trap sounds different; the classifier may confidently assign the wrong species because most training clips came from one setting.

Research question
Can domain-balanced harmonic features and compact learned prototypes improve species recognition when recording settings and species frequencies are confounded?

What you would build/train
If data became accessible: train a pitch-feature forest and small log-mel CNN; compare balanced prototypes and harmonic consistency objectives.

Comparisons
Pitch statistics plus random forest Published challenge baseline Domain-balanced prototypes and domain-adversarial models already present in 2026 results

Evaluation
Hold out complete recording domains and original recording groups; do not scatter adjacent clips across splits. Report supported species-domain cells separately and avoid evaluating missing class/domain combinations as if balanced.

Data and terms
Zenodo metadata and file list read; development archive returned HTTP 403 using ordinary public GET and bounded range requests. No actual audio sample obtained. Zenodo record declares CC BY 4.0. Licence metadata is not proof of usable file access; no qualified topic until a permitted public route actually works.

CPU plan
Unqualified: could start with 5,000 clips and fixed features on CPU, but this plan is not an access proof.

Semester scope
A full study would require confounding audit, matched baselines, controlled balancing, held-domain evaluation and interpretation across several months.

Closest work
BioDCASE 2026 results already include balanced prototypes, harmonic/pitch methods, domain adaptation and large pretrained embeddings.

Proposed contribution
Would need a careful causal-confounding study, not a claim that balancing or pitch features are new.

Research connection
The official master-supervision page explicitly includes audio event/species classification.

Limits
Fit cannot repair the failed data gate; this is not an offered LTU mosquito project. Proposed fit only: no examiner, supervisor, project membership or resource approval.

Risks
Actual files inaccessible Large class/domain imbalance Closely overlapping published approaches

Output
Blocked lead audit; no selectable thesis proposal.

Verdict
Failed actual-data access gate; prior work also removes the original generic adaptation claim.
