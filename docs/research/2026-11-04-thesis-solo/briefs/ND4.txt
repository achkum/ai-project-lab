ND4: Leave room for appliances the energy model has never seen
4 November 2026 — WITHDRAW

Problem
A model can force all electricity use into known appliance labels, making an unseen appliance look like a familiar one.

Example
A heater is plugged in for the first time. A model should not automatically report that the washing machine used that energy.

Research question
Can an explicitly learned unknown-load residual reduce false attribution while preserving useful known-appliance estimates?

What you would build/train
Would train compact sequence-to-point regressors with a nonnegative residual and conservation penalty; compare matched models without the unknown component.

Comparisons
Appliance-state thresholds and factorial hidden Markov baseline Compact sequence-to-point CNN Event-aware physics-constrained NILM and recent transfer/attention approaches

Evaluation
Hold out houses and complete appliance instances; chronological splits within remaining homes. Preserve missing-data indicators and audit filled zeros. Evaluate false attribution, energy conservation and per-appliance errors.

Data and terms
UK-DALE owner page and REFIT publisher/Zenodo metadata read. UK-DALE legacy host redirected away from data; actual cleaned REFIT CSV and publisher files returned 403. UK-DALE/REFIT pages declare CC BY 4.0. A separate Figshare REFIT Smart Home record was inspected but is not the electrical-load dataset and is not treated as substitute access.

CPU plan
Unqualified. A downsampled week and a sub-500k-parameter model could fit CPU, but no usable selected waveform sample was verified.

Semester scope
Would require missingness audit, controlled unseen-appliance construction, model training, cross-house evaluation and uncertainty analysis.

Closest work
Recent physics-aware and event-aware NILM papers already use conservation; October 2026 diffusion NILM is a costly alternative with unavailable-on-request data.

Proposed contribution
Study unknown appliance attribution under realistic missing measurements, rather than claiming conservation itself is new.

Research connection
Efficient energy-related learning could be relevant, but no specific current LTU NILM research connection was verified.

Limits
Failed data access is independently sufficient to withdraw. Proposed fit only: no examiner, supervisor, project membership or resource approval.

Risks
Data download failed Unknown residual may absorb all errors without identifying anything useful No verified bill savings or buyer demand

Output
Blocked data-access record and a scoped research question.

Verdict
Metadata openness did not translate into verified usable data.
